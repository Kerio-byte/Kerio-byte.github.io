UchiSamBot — Study Helper

UchiSamBot is a Telegram bot for school students who want to actually understand their homework — not just copy the answer. It breaks problems down step by step, explains topics in simple language, checks your solution, and makes revision notes and flashcards. Subjects: mathematics, Russian language, physics, chemistry, history. The first 5 breakdowns are free; after that, packs of breakdowns cost a few Telegram Stars. Interface languages: Russian and English.
